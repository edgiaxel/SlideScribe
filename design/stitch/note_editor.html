Understanding Forecasting

Forecasting uses historical observations to estimate future outcomes across business horizons. Organizations rely heavily on dynamic models to balance seasonal trends, short-term demand variations, and macroeconomic shifts slideshow Slide 14 · PPTX to avoid inventory overruns.

“A forecast is an operational estimate, never an absolute guarantee.”

Core Lecture Principle · Prof. Aris Thorne
